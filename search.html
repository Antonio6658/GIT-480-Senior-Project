Trending Hobbies this month

Food

Food is an ever changing hobby that includes a wide range of subsections for food. This includes traditional cooking in the kitchen, baking, frying, grilling, and any other method of food preparation.

Gaming

Board games, video games, chess, tabletop games like DND, are all increasingly popular hobbies in a fantasy world. From PC gaming to Monopoly, any game in this section has increased in popularity over the years. Leading to players even going professional in e-sports, which are competitive video game tournaments.

Creative

Creative hobbies are a more traditional form of entertainment. People draw, paint, make music, pottery, and many other creative methods of expression are popular hobbies for those with a natural eye for creativity and bringing their ideas to life.

Exercise

Movements for working out and exercise has increased greatly over time. More people are lifting weights, training for their next marathon, and keeping their physical fitness in top shape as a hobby.
