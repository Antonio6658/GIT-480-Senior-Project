Welcome to Mentor Connection

Our Mission

At Mentor Connection, we bring together mentors and mentees who share a passion for the same hobbies. We strive to foster lasting relationships, spark creativity, and help you grow your skills alongside like-minded people.

Mentorship

What is a mentorship?

A mentorship is when an experienced individual takes a less experienced individual under their wing and teaches them all about a subject. Mentor's are very important for newer individuals for any hobby. Guitar players can learn from more experienced people. Artists can gain new skills from more skilled artists.

Hobby

What is a hobby?

A hobby is an activity that someone does for enjoyment, entertainment, and for fun. Hobbies can be a wide range of subjects. From playing video games, to running, to playing guitar, to weight lifting. Anything that brings you joy that doesn't compare to other activities is a great candidate for your hobby. Go to our hobby search page to find out more about which one may be right for you?

Meetings

How do we hold meetings?

For meetings on Mentor Connection, we recommend using Zoom for ease of use. While some mentors or mentees might be far away from where you are located, you can still have that connection with other users. Zoom is a useful medium to foster that connection and learn skills from each other. For other users who are closer, you can use Zoom for the initial meeting, then meet up in person to practice your hobbies.
