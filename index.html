Welcome to Mentor Connection

Our Mission

Here at Mentor Connection, our mission is to unite our users with like minded individuals. Hobbies are a method of expression. An escape to a wondrous world where we can lose ourselves in our passions. Sometimes, we want to share that sense of wonder with other individuals who enjoy those same hobbies. That's where Mentor Connection comes in. It is our mission to connect mentors and mentees to foster relationships and grow skills within each person. Mentor Connection is there for you and giving you a chance to grow in your hobbies. Reach out to us if you have any questions or concerns.

Mentorship

What is a mentorship?
Mentorships are when an experienced individual takes a less experienced individual under their wing and teaches them all about a subject. Mentor's are very important for newer individuals for any hobby. Guitar players can learn from more experienced people. Artists can gain new skills from more skilled artists.

Hobby

What is a hobby?
A hobby is an activity that someone does for enjoyment, entertainment, and for fun. Hobbies can be a wide range of subjects. From playing video games, to running, to playing guitar, to weight lifting. Anything that brings you joy that doesn't compare to other activities is a great canidate for your hobby. Go to our hobby search page to find out more about which one may be right for you?

Meetings

How to hold meetings
For meetings on Mentor Connection, we recommend using Zoom for ease of use. While some mentors or mentees might be far away from where you are located, you can still have that connection with other users. Zoom is a useful medium to foster that connection and learn skills from each other. For other users who are closer, you can use Zoom for the initial meeting, then meet up in person to practice your hobbies.
